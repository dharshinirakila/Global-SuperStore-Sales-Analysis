# Global-SuperStore-Sales-Analysis
Global SuperStore Sales Analysis using Power BI , Features sales, product, Regional, customer segment, and Shipping Analysis.

## 📌 Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing Global Superstore sales data using Microsoft Power BI to understand business performance, product sales, regional performance, customer segments, and shipping trends.

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze overall sales performance.
- Identify high-performing product categories and sub-categories.
- Compare sales performance across different regions and states.
- Understand customer segment contributions to sales.
- Analyze shipping methods and order distribution.
- Build interactive dashboards to support business decision-making.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query
- Microsoft Excel / CSV
- Data Cleaning
- Data Analysis
- Data Visualization

## 📊 Dashboard Analysis

### 1. Sales Overview
- Analyzed overall sales performance.
- Compared sales across different product categories.
- Examined regional sales contributions.
- Analyzed sales trends over time.
- Compared sales performance across customer segments.
- Used interactive filters to explore sales data.

### 2. Product Analysis
- This page analyzes product sales and customer orders to understand product performance.
- Top 10 vs. Bottom 10 Products: Identifies the highest-selling and lowest-selling products based on sales.
- Treemap – Regional Sales by Category: Shows sales distribution across regions and product categories.
- Sales by Sub-Category: Compares sales across different product sub-categories to identify top and low performers.
- Customer Orders by Category: Shows the number of customer orders across different product categories.

### 3. Shipping Analysis
- KPI Cards & Region-Category Summary: Displays total states, cities, products, shipping modes, and sales by region and        category.
- Sales Revenue by Ship Mode: Compares sales generated by different shipping methods.
- Regional Sales by Ship Mode: Compares shipping method sales across different regions.
- Monthly Shipping Volume & Sales Trend: Shows monthly sales and shipping trends from January to December.
- Ship Mode Breakdown by Category: Shows shipping methods across different product categories.
- Total Shipments by Region: Compares shipment counts across different regions.
- Geographic Sales Map by State: Visualizes sales distribution across U.S. states to identify high- and low-performing areas.

## 🔍 Key Insights
- Compared sales contributions across different regions.
- Identified product categories and sub-categories with higher sales.
- Examined customer segment contributions to overall sales.
- Analyzed geographical sales distribution across states and cities.
- Explored order distribution and sales performance across shipping methods.
- Used interactive dashboards to explore business data and identify sales patterns.

## 🚀 Conclusion
This project demonstrates the application of Power BI in data cleaning, analysis, and visualization. The interactive dashboards provide insights into sales performance, product categories, regional distribution, customer segments, and shipping trends, helping users understand business data and make informed decisions.
